# 🛡️ Ubika (by ITrust)

**Ubika**, developed by **ITrust**, is a European cybersecurity solution suite offering **vulnerability management**, **compliance monitoring**, and **SOC-as-a-Service** to help organizations maintain strong cyber hygiene and regulatory compliance.

---

## 🔍 Overview

- **Company**: ITrust
- **Product Line**: Ubika
- **Type**: Vulnerability Management & Compliance Platform
- **Headquarters**: France 🇫🇷
- **Focus**:
  - Vulnerability scanning
  - Risk analysis & remediation
  - Compliance audits (ISO 27001, GDPR, NIS2, etc.)
  - SOC-as-a-Service (outsourced monitoring)

---

## 🔧 Core Products

### 📊 Ubika VM (Vulnerability Management)
- Continuous vulnerability scanning
- Asset discovery and mapping
- CVE scoring and risk prioritization
- Customizable remediation plans

### ✅ Ubika Compliance
- Compliance dashboards & reporting
- Prebuilt frameworks: ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, GDPR, NIS2
- Control implementation tracking
- Gap analysis and audit support

### 🔍 Ubika XDR / SIEM / SOC
- Real-time log analysis and threat detection
- 24/7 monitoring by ITrust SOC analysts
- Incident response coordination
- Supports data from firewalls, endpoints, and cloud services

---

## 🚨 Use Cases

- Maintain continuous vulnerability awareness
- Align with regulatory frameworks (GDPR, ISO 27001)
- Outsource SOC operations while retaining visibility
- Get remediation support and reporting for audits

---

## 🤖 Features

- Agentless scanning
- Scans IT, OT, and Cloud assets
- Threat modeling based on CVSS and asset criticality
- Integrates with ticketing systems
- Scalable from SMBs to large enterprises

---

## 🔗 Integrations

- SIEM & EDR systems
- Ticketing tools like Jira, GLPI
- Asset management platforms
- Cloud providers (AWS, Azure)
